About This Dashboard

Understanding How America Grows & Moves

Population growth and vehicle travel are two of the most fundamental indicators of how a society expands and evolves. As states grow — or shrink — their transportation systems must respond. Yet the relationship between how many people live somewhere and how much those people drive is far from simple. It varies by geography, by era, and by economic circumstance.

This dashboard brings together 16 years of annual data — from 2010 through 2025 — on population estimates and vehicle miles traveled (VMT) for all 50 U.S. states and Washington D.C. It is designed to help researchers, planners, policymakers, and curious citizens explore how these two measures have changed over time, how they compare across states, and where driving has grown faster or slower than population.

The period covered spans a remarkable range of conditions: the slow recovery from the 2008 financial crisis, a sustained economic expansion through the mid-2010s, the sharp COVID-19 disruption of 2020 that caused a historic single-year plunge in VMT, and the subsequent rebound through 2025. Together, these years tell a rich and nuanced story about mobility in America.

Explore the Dashboard
Click any tab below to jump straight to that section.
1
Trends & Indexed Growth
Select up to 3 states or the nation and explore raw population and VMT trends on a dual-axis chart. Switch to the indexed view to see relative growth normalized to 1.0 at any base year. An interactive U.S. map lets you visualize population or VMT levels for any year with zoom and pan.
→
2
Per Capita Analysis
Dive into VMT per capita — miles driven per person per year — and compare it against population growth. The indexed view reveals whether Americans are driving more or less per person over time. An interactive map color-codes states by VMT per capita or population level for any chosen year.
→
3
State Comparison
Compare all 50 states and D.C. side by side with a horizontal bar chart showing % change in population, VMT, and VMT per capita over your chosen year range. Sort, filter, and hover for precise values. A choropleth map below lets you visualize which metric changed most across the country.
→
4
Scatter Plot & Quadrant Map
A rich scatter plot places every state by its VMT % change (Y-axis) vs. population % change (X-axis). States where both metrics declined are Black; pop up/VMT down are Green; pop down/VMT up are Red. For states where both grew, four sub-categories (Blue, Brown, Purple, Orange) are determined by whether each state exceeds the median growth rate within that subset. The choropleth map mirrors these seven colors.
→
How to Use This Dashboard
A quick guide to getting the most from the interactive features.
Step 1
Choose your year range
Every analytical tab has dual sliders letting you set a custom start and end year from 2010 to 2025. All charts and maps update instantly.
Step 2
Select geographies
In Tabs 1 and 2, add up to 3 states, D.C., or the U.S. total to compare their trends simultaneously on the same chart.
Step 3
Explore the maps
All maps support scroll-to-zoom, click-and-drag panning, and double-click zoom. Hover over any state for a tooltip with precise values. Use the Reset button to return to full view.
Step 4
Hover for details
In the bar chart and scatter plot, hover over any bar or dot to see a detailed callout with the state name and all relevant statistics for that geography.
2020 COVID Note
All data includes the year 2020. VMT dropped approximately 13% nationally due to COVID-19 pandemic travel restrictions while population remained relatively stable. This creates a visible V-shaped dip in VMT trend lines for that year across nearly every state.

About This Dashboard
America in Motion is an interactive data brief exploring 16 years of population change and vehicle miles traveled (VMT) across all 50 U.S. states and Washington D.C., from 2010 through 2025. The dashboard is designed to make it easy to explore national and state-level trends, compare geographies side by side, examine per capita driving behavior, and understand how population growth and driving have diverged or converged over time.
What's Inside
Tab 1 — Trends & Indexed
Raw dual-axis trends and base-year-indexed growth for population and VMT for up to 3 geographies simultaneously. Interactive U.S. map with year slider, zoom and pan.
Tab 2 — Per Capita
VMT per capita (miles driven per person per year) and indexed per capita trends. Reveals whether Americans are driving more or less per person over time.
Tab 3 — State Comparison
All-state bar chart comparing % change in population, VMT, and VMT per capita over any chosen year range. Sortable and filterable, with a zoomable choropleth map.
Tab 4 — Scatter Plot
A scatter plot of VMT % change (Y) vs. population % change (X) for all states. Each dot is color-coded by quadrant — showing at a glance whether a state has growing or declining population and VMT. A matching choropleth map colors states by their quadrant.

Data Notes
Population figures are July 1 annual estimates and may be revised by the Census Bureau in subsequent vintages. VMT data reflects total annual travel on all public roads and streets. The year 2020 reflects significant COVID-19 pandemic effects — VMT dropped approximately 13% nationally while population remained relatively stable. FHWA periodically revises prior-year estimates; values may differ slightly from older publications.
